## About Me

<div class="about">
<div class="about__text" markdown="1">
Benjamin Mikek is a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Computer Science at Georgia Tech, advised by [Professor Qirun Zhang](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/~qzhang414/). My work lies at the intersection of compilers, formal verification, and agentic AI: I study how to make programs, whether written by humans or LLMs, correct and performant. My work has appeared in top-tier programming-languages conferences, including PLDI and OOPSLA, and won a distinguished paper award at FSE. I have extended my experience with two industry internships at AWS in 2025 and 2026.
